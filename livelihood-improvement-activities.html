    4.2. Eco-development activities

    * Organizing State level workshop for the implementing officers to share the experiences of eco-development in TBGP and discussing the plans for TBGPCCR

    * Organizing Kick-off meetings for the stakeholders to discuss the overall plans for eco development

    * Conducting Field level micro-planning exercises in 155 EDC villages to facilitate participatory planning of ecox0002development plans

    * Constitution of Eco-Development Committees and their Executive Committees

    * Organizing Study tours for the target EDCs to visit other successful groups such as EDCs, VFCs, and SHGs

    * Completion of eco-development plans in each target village by way of addressing socio-economic and ecological requirements

    * Organizing a Nattu vaidyar (traditional medicine practitioner) conference to preserve traditional knowledge among tribal and local people at five places viz. Kolli Hills, Yercaud, Kalrayans, Jamnamarathur and Kallakurichi

    * Heritage conservation at

    a) Kullar Caves in Patramanagalam RF

    b) Gudiyam Caves in Pulikundrum RF

    c) Mel Siddhar Kovil at Kanjamalai RF

    d) Kariyaramar Kovil at Kurumbapatti RF
